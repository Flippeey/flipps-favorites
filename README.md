<h1 align="center">Flipp's Favorites</h1>
<h3 align="center">New Tab Workspaces</h3>

<p align="center">A browser extension that turns your new tab into a fast, visual bookmark workspace. Your existing bookmarks appear instantly — no import required.<br/>Turn any folder into its own themed <strong>Workspace</strong>, search your entire library live, and customise icons, layout, and themes per context.<br/>100% local, no tracking. Works on Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.</p>

## ✨ Features

- 🗂️ **Workspaces** — turn any bookmark folder into its own isolated dashboard, each with its own theme, accent, wallpaper, layout, and dock. Switch contexts in one click, with the desktop jump-menu, or with `Alt` + arrow keys. Create up to 20 Workspaces.
- 🧙 **Onboarding wizard** — scans your bookmarks, detects how you organise, and suggests ready-made Workspaces and layout templates. You are set up in under a minute.
- 🎚️ **Per-Workspace view & sort** — each Workspace remembers its own view mode and sort order, so a reference library and a daily-driver dashboard can each look the way they should.
- 📁 **Create a Workspace from any folder** — promote a folder to a full Workspace from its context menu, or by dragging it onto the Workspace bar.
- 🚀 **The Dock** — a quick-access strip for your most-used folders and sites; always-visible, hover-only, or hidden.
- 🖼️ **Visual tiles with icon control** — automatic favicon detection with a 30-day smart cache; override any icon with a custom image, a URL, or a result from the built-in icon search (which shows where each icon came from).
- 🔍 **Live fuzzy search** — filters across your entire bookmark library instantly from the hero search bar, and understands your folder structure as you type.
- 🎨 **Flexible theming** — light, dark, and system-auto modes; ten hand-crafted accent colours plus a custom colour picker; configurable per Workspace.
- 🌈 **Custom backgrounds** — solid colour, rich gradients (aurora, mesh, vignette, and more), or your own wallpaper with opacity, fit, and position controls.
- 🖱️ **Drag & drop everywhere** — reorder bookmarks and folders, move items between Workspaces, drop into the dock, or resize layout sections; every change applies live.
- ✏️ **Inline editing & folder management** — rename, retag, create, and delete folders directly from the page; no browser bookmark manager required.
- ☑️ **Multi-select** — marquee or `Ctrl`/`Cmd`-click to select many items, then cut, copy, paste, or bulk-delete with confirmation.
- 💾 **Import / export** — back up and restore your full settings and icon overrides as a single JSON file; portable across devices and browsers.
- 🔒 **Privacy-first** — no accounts, no third-party servers, no telemetry, no ads. Requests only the permissions it actually needs.

## 🔐 Privacy

- No accounts, no sign-in, and no servers operated by this extension.
- Your data (settings, Workspaces, icon overrides) lives in your browser's own extension storage. Where your browser provides built-in account sync, preferences sync through it — no third-party cloud is involved.
- Icon resolution uses your sites' own favicons plus Google S2, Icon Horse, and DuckDuckGo as fallbacks — only the hostname of each bookmark is sent, and only when an icon needs resolving.
- No telemetry or analytics.
